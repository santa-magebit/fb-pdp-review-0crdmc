Incarnadine

A classic crimson

Our richest crimson originally takes its name from Latin, but is now a much used term for crimsons and reds. Similar to the deep glossy red used by David Hicks at Barons Court in the 1970s, Incarnadine is unashamedly classic and glamorous.

It can be used to sumptuous effect in halls when offset with Tanner's Brown on woodwork, or feel more edgy and graphic when paired with a bright white.

Key features

  • Unmatchable depth of colour
  • Audited and ethical supply chain
  • Water based with A+ indoor air quality rating
  • Finishes to last: 100% of colours Scrub Class 1 rated*
  • Interior and exterior finishes

Estate Emulsion

Estate Emulsion is our classic matt finish for interior walls and ceilings. Its soft, chalky look gives colour real depth and works beautifully in living rooms, bedrooms and dining rooms.
